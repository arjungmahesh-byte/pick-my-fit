Pick My Fit

Choose what to wear from your own closet. Add clothes from photos or any shop link.

Android

Download for Android
  1. Tap the button. The app file downloads.
  2. Open it from the download message.
  3. If your phone asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.

iPhone

Open the app
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open the app, then the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
